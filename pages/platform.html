Technical questions / 05

Before the first record.

Does Field Record make engineering determinations?

No. The workspace organizes source evidence, candidates, and review states. Final classifications and engineering decisions remain with the appropriately responsible people and processes.

Can output be organized around our asset hierarchy?

Yes. Component and location language is defined during scope design so records can reflect the bridge, corridor, turbine, or other hierarchy used by the owner.

What happens to original inspection media?

Source media remains distinct from annotations and derivative views. The intended retention and delivery requirements are agreed during program design.

Can we review the platform before a field operation?

Yes. A demonstration can begin with a workflow review, then connect the sample workspace to a representative asset and evidence requirement.
